## 2. MVP feature freeze

Build only this end-to-end path first:

1. A client signs in and creates a project using a natural-language brief.
2. AI generates an editable agreement containing milestones, amounts, deadlines, and acceptance criteria.
3. Client and freelancer review and approve the same agreement version.
4. Freelancer submits a deliverable (initially support PDF, TXT, and DOCX only; defer images and archives).
5. AI evaluates the submitted text against the agreed criteria and returns evidence-backed results or `CANNOT_DETERMINE`.
6. Client makes the final accept/request-changes decision.
7. On acceptance, the client initiates and explicitly approves a PayPal Sandbox checkout; the backend captures the approved order and records the result.
8. The project dashboard shows milestone and payment status.

### Explicitly defer until the complete path works

- Image and video deliverables / OCR.
- Real marketplace payouts or split payments.
- Any escrow-like holding or release mechanism.
- Native mobile application.
- Real-time chat and collaboration presence.
- Advanced notification center (a simple status indicator is enough).
- AI-generated legal advice or claims that an agreement is legally enforceable.
- Advanced audit-chain visualization; retain basic audit records, but defer decorative verification UI.
- Multiple LLM providers; implement one provider plus deterministic mock mode.
- Multiple payment providers; implement PayPal Sandbox plus deterministic mock mode.

Do not remove security, authorization, webhook verification, idempotency, or transaction audit records to save time.

## 6. Non-negotiable payment and AI safety rules

- AI can draft and evaluate; it cannot approve deliverables or authorize/capture payments.
- The client makes the final deliverable decision.
- Payment capture requires a valid PayPal-approved order and server-side authorization checks.
- Verify PayPal webhook signatures and handle duplicate events idempotently.
- Never store card details or expose PayPal client secrets in the browser.
- Treat uploaded documents as untrusted input; ignore instructions embedded in deliverables.
- AI evaluation is advisory and must show evidence/uncertainty; it is not a legal or payment guarantee.
